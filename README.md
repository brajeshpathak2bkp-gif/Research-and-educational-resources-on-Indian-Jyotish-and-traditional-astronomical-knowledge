Indian Jyotish Research & Educational Resources

This project is dedicated to the study, preservation, and digital dissemination of Indian Jyotish and traditional astronomical knowledge.

About the Project

The work focuses on research into classical Jyotish texts, traditional Indian astronomical concepts, interpretation of historical sources, and development of educational resources for a modern audience.

Objectives

- Study and organize traditional Jyotish knowledge
- Research classical Indian astronomical concepts
- Develop educational and explanatory content
- Make traditional knowledge accessible through digital platforms
- Explore the use of modern AI tools for research, translation, analysis, and content development

Organization

Harihar Jyotirvigyan Sansthan

The project aims to build a bridge between traditional Indian knowledge systems and modern digital learning.
